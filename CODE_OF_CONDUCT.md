# Code of Conduct

Lockstep Dev follows a simple contribution standard: be technical, specific,
and respectful.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss code and design decisions directly.
- Provide reproducible bug reports where possible.
- Respect the open-source boundary between Dev and private Lockstep Inference
  work.
- Avoid harassment, personal attacks, spam, and disclosure of private data.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove issues, comments, or contributions that are abusive,
off-topic, or unsafe. Serious reports can be sent to security@lockstepai.dev.
